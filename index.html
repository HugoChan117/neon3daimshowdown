ORB WIKI

85% of spawns are normal blue targets · 10% are risk orbs · 5% are rewards.
Every orb has a unique color. Combo raises target value · missing or hitting a risk orb resets your combo.
BULLSEYE: hit the inner 30% of any orb's area to increase its effect.

HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

Shoot neon orbs to score points before your personal clock runs out. The highest score when the round ends wins.

CONTROLS

  • 1P — Mouse aim · Click to fire · Space fallback
  • 2P — P1: Mouse aim + Click to fire · P2: Arrows arm + Enter/Shift/Ctrl fire
  • 3P — P3: WASD aim + F fire
  • 4P — P4: IJKL aim + P fire
  • Mobile — Tap to aim & fire (1P only)
  • Esc — Pause · Sound button — Mute

SCORING

  • Normal target: 100 pts + 10 per combo (max combo ×20)
  • Combo resets on a miss or on a risk orb
  • BULLSEYE — hit the inner orb area to increase the effect of any orb.

YOUR TIMER

  • Each player has an individual clock. When it hits 0 you are out.
  • Time shards add +3s to your clock.
  • Freeze orb pauses your clock for 4s.
  • The round ends when all players are out.
