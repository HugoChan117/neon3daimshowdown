ORB WIKI

85% of spawns are normal blue targets · 10% are risk orbs (trap / tax, split 2:1) · 5% are rewards (time / multi / freeze).
Every orb has a unique color. Combo raises target value · missing or hitting a risk orb resets your combo.

HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

Shoot neon orbs to score points before your personal clock runs out. The highest score when the round ends wins.

CONTROLS

  • 1P — Mouse / WASD / Arrows aim · Click / Space fire
  • 2P — P1: WASD + Space/F · P2: Arrows + Enter
  • 3P — P1: WASD + Space/F · P2: Arrows + Enter · P3: Mouse + Click
  • Esc / P — Pause · Sound button — Mute

SCORING

  • Blue target: 100 pts + 10 per combo (max combo ×20)
  • Combo resets on a miss or on a risk orb
  • Multiplier orb: ×3 points for 5s

YOUR TIMER

  • Each player has an individual clock. When it hits 0 you are out.
  • Time shards add +4s to your clock.
  • Freeze orb pauses your clock for 3s.
  • The round ends when all players are out.

RISK ORBS

  • Red Trap — −150 pts and your combo resets.
  • Purple Tax — instantly lose 30% of your score · for 7s all points earned are halved.

REWARD ORBS

  • Yellow Time — +4s on your clock.
  • Green Multi — ×3 points for 5s.
  • Turquoise Freeze — your clock pauses for 3s.
